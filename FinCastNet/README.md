**FinCastNet – Sentiment-Augmented Stock Prediction System**

FinCastNet is a stock market prediction system that combines financial news sentiment with historical market data and technical indicators to generate more informed stock movement predictions.

**Overview**

Traditional stock prediction models mainly rely on historical prices and technical indicators. However, stock prices are also strongly influenced by news, market sentiment, company announcements, and investor reactions.

FinCastNet addresses this limitation by combining:

Financial news sentiment

Historical stock price data

Technical indicators

Transformer-based contextual features
